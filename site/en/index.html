SONY A6000 · CFW 3.30–4.04 · ELECTRONIC SHUTTER

The A6000 shoots with no mechanical shutter

Modified firmware 3.30 and later shoot with the electronic shutter on the real camera; 3.77 levels the exposure without a RAM patch. The open limits are EFCS (black from 1/1000) and, from 4.01 on, recorded video that the camera will not play. Below, the public index of evidence from the firmware analysis.

LIMITS

What is not solved

FILM-PB-1: with the CFW, clips play on a PC but not on the camera; the likely cause is the PAL/NTSC selector against the clip frame rate, and that setting is not exposed in the menu. EFCS: black from 1/1000 (≈1.27 ms), also with Sony firmware; the cause is physical.
